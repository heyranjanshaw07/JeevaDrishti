Problem Statement

The Challenge in Modern Microscopy

Why conventional approaches fail
🔬
10,000+
Cells Per Slide
Experts manually inspect tens of thousands of cells — time-consuming and prone to fatigue-induced error.
🚫
Closed-Vocabulary Models
Standard CNNs (YOLO, Faster R-CNN) fail completely when encountering novel or rare cell phenotypes.
🌎
Pathologist Shortage
In LMICs, pathologist-to-population ratios can reach 1 : 1,000,000 — manual screening is not scalable.
🌟
Staining & Domain Shift
Different stains (Giemsa, H&E, Papanicolaou) and modalities (brightfield, phase-contrast, fluorescence) cause accuracy collapse.
🏷
Annotation Bottleneck
Pixel-level labelling of dense cell images requires hundreds of expert-hours — cost-prohibitive and unscalable.
⚕
Autonomous AI Risk
Black-box classification without spatial evidence or human oversight creates unacceptable risk in clinical settings.
